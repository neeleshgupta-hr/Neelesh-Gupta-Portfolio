REC/05 Case studies

HR Case Studies

Illustrative Case Studies. These are hypothetical scenarios showing how I approach each HR function. They are not actual employer projects and contain no confidential or identifiable employee information.

CASE 01 · ILLUSTRATIVEEmployee Relations & Workplace Investigations
Situation
An employee raises a concern involving a policy exception and a manager's decision.
Business challenge
Respond fairly and consistently while protecting confidentiality.
HR investigation
Gather facts from case notes and records, identify the applicable policy, and check for precedent or similar exceptions.
Actions taken
Document findings, clarify the policy position with the employee, and escalate to the HRBP where discretion or risk exists.
Stakeholder collaboration
Site HR, HRBP, manager.
Policy & compliance
Policy interpretation, confidentiality, consistent application, documented rationale.
Outcome
A documented, policy-aligned resolution with clear communication and an escalation trail.
Competencies demonstrated
Employee relations, policy interpretation, escalation management, discretion.
CASE 02 · ILLUSTRATIVEPayroll Discrepancy & Root-Cause Investigation
Situation
An employee reports an underpayment after a shift change and transfer.
Business challenge
Correct the pay and prevent a repeat.
HR investigation
Compare timecards, pay codes and attendance with HRIS data in ADP Payforce to find where the data diverged.
Actions taken
Correct at source, request pay adjustment through the proper channel, and record the cause.
Stakeholder collaboration
Payroll, Site HR, employee.
Policy & compliance
Pay accuracy, audit trail, confidentiality of pay data.
Outcome
Accurate correction and a recorded root cause that informs a prevention step.
Competencies demonstrated
Payroll validation, reconciliation, RCA, attention to detail.
CASE 03 · ILLUSTRATIVEEmployee Onboarding & Lifecycle Management
Situation
Several new hires start next week with incomplete records.
Business challenge
Be ready for Day 1 without compliance gaps.
HR investigation
Review pending clearances and HRIS data against the onboarding checklist.
Actions taken
Chase missing documents, set up HRIS records, confirm readiness with the hiring manager.
Stakeholder collaboration
New hires, hiring manager, Recruitment, Site HR.
Policy & compliance
Required documentation, background and verification clearances.
Outcome
Complete records and Day 1 readiness.
Competencies demonstrated
Onboarding, data accuracy, follow-through, coordination.
