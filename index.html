REC/03 Professional summary

HR Operations, Built on Process and Compliance

Who I Am

An HR Operations professional who ran HR Shared Services for a 30,000+ employee population at Amazon across Fulfillment Centers, Sortation Centers, and corporate sites - MBA-qualified, five years in, still closing the gap between policy and practice.

What I Deliver

End-to-end ownership of payroll and timekeeping corrections, FMLA/ADA/STD/PFL leave administration, employee lifecycle transactions, and HRIS data integrity - at case volumes 40% above team average, without giving up quality.

How I Work

I treat every case as two problems: the employee's immediate need, and the process gap that let it happen. Closing only the first creates repeat work; closing both builds workforce continuity and reduces next quarter's volume.

Business Impact

Sustained SLA and quality attainment, audit-ready compliance documentation, fewer repeat escalations, and knowledge assets - SOPs, dashboards, coaching - that shorten ramp-up for the people around me.

"Great HR Operations isn't measured by cases closed - it's measured by the confidence employees and the business place in the support they receive."

Key strengths. Governance and policy integrity under high case volume; root-cause problem solving that turns individual cases into process fixes; cross-functional stakeholder management across HR Business Partners, Payroll, Benefits, Operations, and Safety; and a continuous-improvement mindset that treats every recurring issue as an unbuilt SOP.

How I Approach HR Operations

Process First: Finding the Root Cause

Every case tells a story. I don't just focus on closing requests, I focus on understanding why an issue occurred in the first place. Whether it's resolving an individual payroll discrepancy or identifying patterns across recurring cases, my approach is to analyze the root cause, improve processes, and prevent similar issues from happening again.

Compliance With Consistency and Care

HR compliance is built on accuracy, fairness, and trust. I approach every compliance-related case with the same level of rigor and attention to detail. Whether handling FMLA, ADA, STD, or PFL cases, I ensure policies are applied consistently because the greatest risks often exist within exceptions and overlooked details.

Every Case Represents a Person

Behind every case number is an employee's paycheck, their leave, their wellbeing, or their return-to-work journey. Even in high-volume HR environments, I believe operational rigor must always be balanced with empathy. Every interaction is an opportunity to support employees while delivering accurate and timely solutions.

Data-Driven Decisions: Turning Cases Into Insights

HR operations is more than resolving individual requests, it is about identifying trends and creating meaningful improvements. By analyzing case patterns, operational metrics, and recurring challenges, I focus on finding opportunities to improve processes, reduce repeat issues, and enhance the overall workforce experience.

Ownership From Start to Resolution

I believe accountability goes beyond completing assigned tasks. I take ownership of understanding the issue, collaborating with stakeholders, communicating effectively, and ensuring every case reaches the right resolution while maintaining quality and compliance.

Continuous Improvement Mindset

Every process has room for improvement. I continuously look for ways to simplify workflows, eliminate inefficiencies, and build scalable solutions that make HR operations more effective for both employees and business teams.

Situation

An employee contacts HR during a critical moment - a payroll discrepancy, a leave request (FMLA/ADA/STD/PFL), a workplace accommodation, a benefits question, or a return-to-work update. Every one of these moments carries real weight for the employee and real compliance exposure for the business.

01 · CHALLENGE

Speed, Accuracy, and Compliance - All at Once

Every case requires urgency and empathy on one side, and accuracy and policy compliance on the other. Treating it as a simple ticket risks a wrong decision, a compliance gap, or a repeat issue down the line.

02 · ACTIONS

Investigate, Then Coordinate

I start by investigating the full context - HRIS records, timecards, leave and benefits documentation, prior case history, and applicable policy - to identify the root cause, not just the symptom. From there, I bring in the right partners:

  • HR Business Partners, Payroll, and Benefits Administration
  • Leave & Disability Management and Site HR
  • Operations Leadership and Safety Teams, when needed
03 · OUTCOME

Resolved Accurately, Documented Fully

The case is resolved, the employee is kept informed throughout, policy and legal compliance are maintained, and every action is documented for audit readiness - so the resolution holds up after the fact, not just at closure.

04 · BUSINESS IMPACT

Value Beyond the Individual Case

  • Timely, accurate support and clear communication
  • Confidence the concern was handled fairly
For the business
  • Accurate records and policy adherence
  • Reduced operational risk and stronger stakeholder partnership
  • Root causes documented to prevent recurrence
