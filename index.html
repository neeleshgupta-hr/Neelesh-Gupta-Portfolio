REC/01 Professional summary

HR Operations, Built on Process and Compliance

Every employee interaction has a story behind it.

It could be a new hire preparing for their first day, an employee concerned about a missing paycheck, someone navigating a medical leave, or a manager seeking guidance on an HR policy. Behind every question is a person looking for clarity, accuracy, and support, and that's where my role began.

For over five years at Amazon, I worked in HR Operations and HR Shared Services, supporting more than 30,000 U.S.-based employees across Fulfillment Centers, Sortation Centers, and corporate sites. Working in a fast-paced, high-volume environment meant no two days were ever the same. One case might involve investigating payroll or timekeeping discrepancies, while the next could require reviewing leave documentation, coordinating workplace accommodations, updating employee records, processing shift changes, or resolving complex HR issues related to payroll, benefits, attendance, leave administration, and the employee lifecycle.

What kept the work interesting wasn't simply resolving cases, it was understanding the people and processes behind them. Every situation required careful analysis, sound judgment, and a consistent approach to applying company policies while balancing business needs with the employee experience. Success depended on close collaboration with HR Business Partners, Site HR, Payroll, Benefits, Operations, Safety, and other cross-functional teams to ensure every resolution was accurate, compliant, and delivered on time.

Over the years, I realized that great HR Operations isn't measured by the number of cases you close - it's measured by the confidence employees have in the support they receive. Accuracy builds trust. Compliance protects the business. Strong processes create consistency. Together, they form the foundation of an exceptional employee experience.

Those principles continue to shape how I approach my work today: solving complex problems with empathy, improving processes through continuous learning, and delivering HR solutions that are accurate, compliant, and genuinely employee-focused.

How I Approach HR Operations

Process First: Finding the Root Cause

Every case tells a story. I don't just focus on closing requests, I focus on understanding why an issue occurred in the first place. Whether it's resolving an individual payroll discrepancy or identifying patterns across recurring cases, my approach is to analyze the root cause, improve processes, and prevent similar issues from happening again.

Compliance With Consistency and Care

HR compliance is built on accuracy, fairness, and trust. I approach every compliance-related case with the same level of rigor and attention to detail. Whether handling FMLA, ADA, STD, or PFL cases, I ensure policies are applied consistently because the greatest risks often exist within exceptions and overlooked details.

Every Case Represents a Person

Behind every case number is an employee's paycheck, their leave, their wellbeing, or their return-to-work journey. Even in high-volume HR environments, I believe operational excellence must always be balanced with empathy. Every interaction is an opportunity to support employees while delivering accurate and timely solutions.

Data-Driven Decisions: Turning Cases Into Insights

HR operations is more than resolving individual requests, it is about identifying trends and creating meaningful improvements. By analyzing case patterns, operational metrics, and recurring challenges, I focus on finding opportunities to improve processes, reduce repeat issues, and enhance the overall employee experience.

Ownership From Start to Resolution

I believe accountability goes beyond completing assigned tasks. I take ownership of understanding the issue, collaborating with stakeholders, communicating effectively, and ensuring every case reaches the right resolution while maintaining quality and compliance.

Continuous Improvement Mindset

Every process has room for improvement. I continuously look for ways to simplify workflows, eliminate inefficiencies, and build scalable solutions that make HR operations more effective for both employees and business teams.

Supporting Employees Through Critical Moments

Every HR case represents an important moment in an employee's journey.

Whether it's a payroll issue, a leave of absence, a workplace accommodation, a benefits inquiry, or a return-to-work request, HR Operations plays a vital role in ensuring employees receive timely, accurate, and compliant support. My focus is on resolving issues efficiently while creating a positive employee experience.

01 · THE EMPLOYEE NEED

An Employee Reaches Out During a Critical Moment

An employee contacts HR with an issue affecting an important stage of their employment journey. This could include:

  • A payroll discrepancy
  • A leave of absence request (FMLA, ADA, STD, or PFL)
  • A workplace accommodation request
  • A benefits or eligibility concern
  • A return-to-work or employment status update
  • An HRIS or attendance discrepancy

Each situation is unique, but every case requires urgency, empathy, accuracy, and compliance.

02 · THE INVESTIGATION

Understanding the Full Employee Story

Rather than addressing only the immediate issue, I investigate the complete context by reviewing relevant systems, documentation, and policies. This may include:

  • HRIS records and employee history
  • Timecards, attendance, and payroll data
  • Leave documentation and supporting records
  • Benefits eligibility and enrollment details
  • Accommodation requests and interactive process documentation
  • Internal policies and compliance requirements
  • Previous case history and related employee interactions

The objective is to identify the root cause while ensuring every decision is accurate, compliant, and aligned with company policy.

03 · THE RESOLUTION

Coordinating Across HR to Deliver the Right Outcome

Many HR cases require collaboration across multiple teams. I partner with the appropriate stakeholders to ensure employees receive complete and timely support. Depending on the situation, this may involve:

  • HR Business Partners
  • Payroll
  • Benefits Administration
  • Leave & Disability Management
  • Site HR
  • Operations Leadership
  • Safety Teams

Whether correcting payroll, processing leave, coordinating workplace accommodations, resolving benefits issues, or supporting an employee's return to work, the priority remains the same:

  • Resolve the issue accurately.
  • Communicate clearly with the employee.
  • Maintain policy and legal compliance.
  • Document every action for audit readiness.
04 · THE IMPACT

Supporting People While Strengthening Processes

A successful resolution delivers value beyond closing a case.

For the employee
  • Timely and accurate support
  • Clear communication throughout the process
  • Confidence that their concerns have been addressed fairly
  • A positive experience during an important lifecycle event
For the business
  • Accurate payroll and HR records
  • Policy and compliance adherence
  • Reduced operational risk
  • Strong cross-functional collaboration
  • Improved process consistency
  • Root causes documented to prevent recurring issues
